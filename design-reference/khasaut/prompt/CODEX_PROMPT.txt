- mountain background behind everything;
- overlaps and rotations similar to reference;
- tag overlaps front/lower collage.

Do not hardcode one fixed 1440px composition.

Recommended hero layer behavior:
- photo 1: enters first from upper-right;
- photo 2: enters from left/lower-left;
- photo 3: enters from lower-center;
- photo 4: enters from right;
- tag enters last with a slightly stronger rotational settle.

Motion:
- staggered transform + opacity;
- tasteful spring/cubic-bezier;
- tiny pointer parallax on desktop;
- tag can lag a little behind pointer;
- use transform only where possible;
- no layout thrashing;
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Keep motion premium and restrained.
No excessive bouncing, wobbling or perpetual movement.
